Billing questions

How does the 7-day free trial work?

Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial. You can cancel during the trial and you won't be charged. If you don't cancel, billing starts automatically when the trial ends.

Can I switch between monthly and annual?

Yes. You can change your billing interval at any time from the customer portal; the change is prorated.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep access until the end of your current billing period. Your scan history and projects are preserved, and you can resubscribe anytime.

What is your refund policy?

You may request a full refund within 14 days of your purchase, for any reason. Read the full Refund Policy.

Who charges my card?

Paddle.com — our online reseller and Merchant of Record. Paddle handles payment processing, tax calculation, and invoices. See our Terms of Service.
